About

A small thread, sent digitally

Raksha Bandhan is about a thread tied by hand — but siblings aren't always in the same city, or even the same country, on the day itself. Rakhi Memory is a small way to close that gap: a personalised card built from your names and a real photo, ready to send in under a minute, for the price of a cup of chai.

How it works

You fill in two names, upload a photo, and pick one of three designs. We generate a high-resolution card right in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server except the payment itself. Once you've paid ₹10, you can download the card or send it straight to WhatsApp.

Frequently asked

Is my photo stored anywhere?

No. The card is drawn entirely in your browser using your device's own processing — your photo never leaves your device except inside the image you choose to download or share.

What payment methods are supported?

Any UPI app — PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, and others — plus cards and net banking through our payment partner.

Can I get a refund?

Since the ₹10 fee unlocks a digital product generated instantly, it isn't refundable once the card has been generated — but if a payment was deducted and the card didn't unlock, reach out and we'll sort it out.
