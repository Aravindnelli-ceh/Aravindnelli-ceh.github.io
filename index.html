How it works

Set up once, review drafts daily.

  • Connect your review sources

    Link your Google Business Profile, Yelp and Facebook Page. ReviewPilot pulls new reviews automatically.

  • Tell it how you sound

    Pick a tone and give two or three example replies you like. It learns your voice, not a generic template.

  • Approve or edit each draft

    Every reply is a draft, never auto-posted without you. Approve in one tap, or tweak the wording first.

  • Watch the trend, not just the star count

    Weekly summary shows which locations and which topics (wait times, pricing, staff) are driving sentiment.
